How to study: 6 stages, 4 lanes in parallel

There are no fixed days. Each study day you work in all four lanes at once, and you move to the next stage when you pass its "move on when" check. Tap a number to open that topic.

  • Only the Core lane waits for the check. Coding, Light and Build keep going at their own pace, even across stages.
  • A stage takes as long as you need. Moving on early, before you can pass the check, is what makes interviews go wrong.
  • Every few days, re-take a short quiz on the MCQ topics you already finished, so they stay fresh.
